# Sharing and rights

Back to [[00 Welcome|Welcome]].

## Members of a space

Right-click a space, then **Members and settings**. Invite someone there, with a link to copy or by mail, and give them a right:

| Right | may |
| --- | --- |
| Read | read and search the notes |
| Write | also make, change, move and delete |
| Manage | also invite, give rights, share and rename the space |

What someone may not read, nexlore gives away nowhere: not on the map, not in the search, not in the backlinks.

## Public pages

Whoever manages a space can share a note or a folder as a reading page, with an expiry and a password if wanted. The **Share** button shows at the top of the note once the operator has allowed public pages. Properties and %%comments%% stay at home.

## Two people, one note

While someone edits a note, everyone else sees a hint and can read. If somebody changes the same file outside nexlore at the same time, nobody loses anything: nexlore makes a conflict copy and shows both side by side.
