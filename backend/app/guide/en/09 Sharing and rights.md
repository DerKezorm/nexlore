# Sharing and rights

Back to [[00 Welcome|Welcome]].

## Members of a space

Right-click a space, then **Members and settings**. Invite someone there, with a link to copy, by mail or with the name of an existing account, and give them a right. Whoever is invited by name finds the invitation under **New since your last visit** and accepts or declines it.

| Right | may |
| --- | --- |
| Read | read and search the notes |
| Write | also make, change, move and delete |
| Manage | also invite, give rights, share and rename the space |

What someone may not read, nexlore gives away nowhere: not on the map, not in the search, not in the backlinks.

## Public pages

Whoever manages a space can share a note or a folder as a reading page, with an expiry and a password if wanted. The **Share** button shows at the top of the note once the operator has allowed public pages. Properties and %%comments%% stay at home.

## Two people, one note

At the top of a note you see who has it open right now. While someone edits a note, everyone else sees a hint and can read. If somebody changes the same file outside nexlore at the same time, nobody loses anything: nexlore makes a conflict copy and shows both side by side.

## Comments

Mark some words and choose **Comment**. The thread stands in the **Comments** tab beside the note, not in the file. Whoever may read may comment. With `@name` you address a member; they see it under **New since your last visit**.

## How others see you

Under **My account → Profile** you give yourself a **display name**, for example with first and last name. It shows wherever you appear. In member lists and in the `@` suggestions your sign-in name stays beside it in small letters, because that is what goes into the text after `@`.

## Notifications

Under **My account → Notifications** you choose what you hear about: names and answers, invitations, approvals for AI programs and, once a day, your tasks that are due. They go to a **webhook** of your choosing, a notification inbox for example, and by **mail** when the operator set up a mail server. **Send a test** shows at once whether it arrives.

> [!note] What a notification says
> The name of the note and who did something, never what a note or a comment says.
