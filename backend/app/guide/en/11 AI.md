# AI

Back to [[00 Welcome|Welcome]].

nexlore knows two ways for AI. Both are off from the start; the operator switches them on under Settings → Server → AI and plugins.

## AI in notes

Every account connects its own AI service under **My account → AI in notes**: an address, a key, a model. Tiles fill in the address of well-known services; a service at home like Ollama works just as well.

Then the editor shows **AI** in its toolbar and its right-click menu:

- Correct spelling
- Rewrite, in nine tones from "More formal" to "Plain language"
- Translate
- Summarize
- Write for me, with the note as material

> [!important] Nothing changes by itself
> The result always stands next to your text first. Only **Take it over** changes the note, and undo brings the old text back. Which texts went out, you see word for word under "What went out", for 14 days.

## AI from outside (MCP)

Programs on your computer, an AI assistant for example, read your notes over MCP. Make the key for it under **My account**; the lines to paste into the program are there too. A key may read, propose drafts or write, and never more than your account. Drafts show at the top of the note to compare and take over.
