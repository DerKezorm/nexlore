# AI

Back to [[00 Welcome|Welcome]].

nexlore knows two ways for AI. Both are off from the start; the operator switches them on under Settings → Server → AI and plugins.

## AI in notes

Every account connects its own AI service under **My account → Connections → AI in notes**: an address, a key, a model. Tiles fill in the address of well-known services; a service at home like Ollama works just as well.

Then the editor shows **AI** in its toolbar and its right-click menu:

- Correct spelling
- Rewrite, in nine tones from "More formal" to "Plain language"
- Translate
- Summarize
- Write for me, with the note as material

> [!important] Nothing changes by itself
> The result always stands next to your text first. Only **Take it over** changes the note, and undo brings the old text back. Which texts went out, you see word for word under "What went out", for 14 days.

## AI from outside (MCP)

Programs on your computer, an agent or an AI app work with your notes over MCP: search, read, propose drafts, make, change, move, comment. Never more than your account may. You connect them under **My account → AI**:

- **With a key**, for programs with a fixed header. The lines to paste into the program are there too.
- **As a connector**, for programs that sign in themselves: they need only the address of nexlore with `/api/mcp` after it. You sign in, choose the level and the spaces, and agree.

Under each key, **Rights per tool** sets what the program may do: **Allow** runs at once, **Ask** waits for you, **Deny** hides the tool altogether. At first reading is allowed, making and changing asks, deleting, sharing and members are denied.

> [!tip] Approvals
> When a program asks, the request stands under "New since your last visit" and in the account menu under **Approvals**, with exactly what it sent. **Approve and run** runs exactly that, **Decline** nothing. After 24 hours the request runs out.

Drafts show at the top of the note to compare and take over.
